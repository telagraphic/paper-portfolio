# Sells lock in gains, and a deletion erases a mistake

A sell is how a gain becomes realized. It stays in the book, returns cash, and reduces shares. A deletion is only for a trade recorded by mistake. It removes that buy or sell and rebuilds the book as if the trade never happened.

Buys-only was the previous rule: the way to undo a purchase was to delete it, and sells did not exist. That kept a single gesture for leaving a position and for fixing a typo, and it dropped the gain when the shares left. The two gestures are different. Selling 4 of 10 shares is a real exit. Deleting the sell is the admission that the exit was typed in error.

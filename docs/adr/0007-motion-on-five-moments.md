# Motion stays on five moments

ADR 0006 sets where each region sits. This sets how that page moves. Motion is concentrated on five moments, and `prefers-reduced-motion` turns all five off. Chrome is CSS. Book figures and the listing table count up once with Number Flow on a cold load. Listing figures roll with Number Flow when the selected listing changes. Cash still morphs with Torph, and only when its previous string is still in memory. The panel fade is the entrance. A figure animation does not replay every time a tab is shown.

The tab pill moves by left and width, 200ms ease-out, and the move can be interrupted. It does not travel in from the left on first paint. The panel fades over 150ms. It does not slide horizontally, so the chart measures a stable column. Search/Manual and Buy/Sell are the same kind of segment. Search/Manual moves the pill with the mode. Buy stays the filled side of its pair. Sell sits in that track and still acts immediately. Those two do not scale on press.

The chart fades in when it mounts. There is no fill behind it. This runs on a tab change and when the selected listing changes. Buy/Sell has no chart. The series itself stays still.

Listing rows and search rows change background in about 100ms. Hover leaves. Selection stays. There is no lift and no shadow. A new listing enters once when the list is shown. An existing listing updates in place. A sold-out listing stays in the list at zero shares and does not play an exit.

Equity, day gain, total gain, and realized gain count up with Number Flow on a cold load, then roll when those values change. Cash morphs with Torph. Listing figures roll with Number Flow when a row is chosen. The order total under the trade form stays still.

Search, Buy, and Sell stay in place and stay busy until the request ends in hits, a status, or an error. The busy state is the control's own label. It is not a spinner. Search results fade in over 150ms, and the rows do not stagger.

A button that is not a segment scales to 0.96 on press. Notes, errors, and status fade in. Delete confirmation is a short fade, and it is not one of the five. History rows do not enter one by one.

The alternatives were a recipe pack, digit drums, spinners, and a Motion-and-Tailwind segmented control. transitions.dev's polish notes are the timing reference, and that skill stays uninstalled. Its recipe pack, its digit drums, and its generation tools stay unused. loading.dev, kugiri, and beautifului.dev do not fit this page. interior.dev's traveling pill is the interaction reference, and the pill is a CSS transition of left and width rather than that kit. Number Flow rolls the digits and yields to reduced motion. Torph remains the cash morph because it needs no second copy of the book.

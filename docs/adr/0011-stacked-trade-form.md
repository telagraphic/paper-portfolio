# The trade form is a stacked column, and its fields move with Motion

The Buy/Sell tab is two columns. The left column is the order, on a white card with the tab tracks' radius. Search/Manual and Buy/Sell share its first line at half the width each, on a gray track, so the settings read apart from the fields. The fields are stacked one per row at one width, filled light gray, and their border darkens on focus or press. A single submit button named for the side closes the card. The right column holds Cash and Total, and the listings a buy search finds. The form is anchored to the top of the panel, and the left column has a fixed width, so changing side or mode only moves what sits below the fields.

Buy/Sell is now state, not two submit buttons. Buy searches or takes a typed symbol and exchange. Sell picks from a dropdown of the listings the book holds and fills the share price from that listing's mark. Search/Manual sits out while Sell is chosen. The Buy/Sell pill takes the green and red of the side pills on Totals and History, `--color-up` for Buy and `--color-down` for Sell, and Search/Manual keeps the black pill. Search and Manual each carry a short tooltip on hover and focus, Automatic ticker lookup and Add the ticker manually. Manual's Symbol label links to TradingView for looking up a symbol and its exchange. A manual buy is priced in dollars, so the form has no currency selector. This amends ADR 0007, where Buy stayed the filled side of its pair and Sell acted immediately.

Buy has four fields and Sell has three, and that change is a sixth motion moment. Exchange opens by making room over 480ms on a long ease-out and then fading in with a slight blur. It closes the other way round, fading out first and then giving the room back over 420ms. Its gap moves with its height, so the button and notes below glide instead of jumping. The first field, whether Search, Symbol, or Listing, cross-fades in place over 360ms, the old and new fields overlapping, so the slot is never empty. The submit button follows Torph's own action demo. Its label names the order as it fills in, from Buy to Buy 10 to Buy 10 AAPL, and morphs over 600ms on a slight overshoot. While the trade is sent, a spinner springs in beside Buying 10 AAPL. When it lands, a check replaces the spinner and the label reads Bought 10 AAPL for 1.8 seconds before it settles back to the next order. The icon slot opens and the icon scales up from 0.6 on a heavy spring, mass 4, stiffness 800, and damping 80. The label's edges are masked so letters fade at the sides. These run slower and softer than the 150ms to 200ms chrome, because a field changing under the cursor read as harsh at that speed. `prefers-reduced-motion` sets all of it to no duration and stops the spinner.

Motion (`motion/react`) does the field animation, because Torph morphs text and cannot animate an element entering or leaving. ADR 0007 turned down a Motion segmented control. The segments still move by CSS, and Motion is used only where height has to animate from and to `auto`. A CSS grid-rows transition was the alternative. It needs the field kept in the DOM while hidden, which leaves an Exchange input that still takes focus and submits.
